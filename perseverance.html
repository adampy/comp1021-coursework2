Perseverance

The most recent rover to be sent to Mars from NASA is called Perseverance. It was called Perseverance after a naming contest that included schools across the US, with children suggesting names for the rover.

It began its journey to Mars on 30th July 2020 and landed on Mars after about seven months in transit in Space on 18th February 2021. Not only was Perseverance sent to Mars, but also a small robotic helicopter called Ingenuity. The objective of the rover and helicopter are to investigate whether there are signs of habitable conditions in Mars' past.

Audio Recordings

The Perseverance rover has two microphones on it, and is the first rover to have recorded sound from another planet. As a result of the different atmosphere on Mars (composed of a lot more carbon dioxide), some sounds seem distorted.

A 20-second clip of the wind on Mars. Rover sounds were removed from the recording by NASA engineers. Wind can be heard towards the end of the recording.

The 18-second clip contains the first recorded audio from another planet. It is the raw audio recorded two days after landing on Mars. The main sound that can be heard is that of the rover, but this allowed engineers to understand how sound dissipates in a different atmosphere.

Mars Samples

The Perseverance rover is continously collecting and storing samples that will, in the future, be collected by a seperate ship and brought back to Earth. These samples will undergo a deep analysis by the NASA scientists and will contribute enormously to the global effort to understand the history of the Red Planet.[2]
