Voyager 2

Voyager 2 is a space probe that was launced in 1977, with a main goal of studying the outer planets of the Solar System, and deep outer Space. The space probe as now been operating for 44 years but most of the probe's systems aren't in operation due to power constraints.

The Grand Tour program was a program devised by NASA in which space probes visit every planet in the outer Solar System, and this was replaced by Voyager 1 and Voyager 2 launches.[1]. Where is Voyager 2 now?

Voyager 2, currently, is in interstellar space, and the main objective of the mission has changed from investigating the outer Solar System to interstellar space. Some of its instruments have been disabled in order to conserve energy - they don't have solar panels because they are so far away from the Sun that they would never work! [4]
